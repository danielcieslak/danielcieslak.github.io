I'm a Data Analytics Engineer at Tesla in Berlin, where I build the data pipelines, dashboards, and reporting models that turn raw operational data into decisions for a workforce of more than 11,500 people. I trained as a Production Engineer, which gave me a solid quantitative foundation in mathematics, probability, and statistics.

Most of my days are spent turning raw operational data into reliable, decision-ready analysis. Along the way I've built Power Apps automations that replaced manual reporting workflows and cut that effort by more than 90%, and I've learned how much further I could take my foundation with rigorous, formal study in statistical methods.

I'm continuing to deepen my grounding in statistics, R, and data science while applying it directly in industry. I've lived and studied across Brazil, Italy, and Germany, and I speak four languages. I'm currently open to opportunities in Data Analytics and Data Science, and open to relocation.

The tools I reach for most: SQL, Python, TypeScript, Power BI, Power Apps, Tableau, Salesforce, and MongoDB / PostgreSQL.
